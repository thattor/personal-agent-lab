# N1-01 reconciliation under D029

## 1. Compatibility and decision class

**Are they incompatible?** Yes, but only for underspecified requests. The old N1-01 metric scored "recognized request" as "exactly one Goal on turn 1." D029, which follows the owner's direct instruction, makes asking an essential question before creating a Goal the correct behavior when content is missing. Take "Please make a draft without sending it." It has no purpose, no recipient and no facts. The literal metric would reward creating a Goal with no content, which the owner explicitly replaced. For sufficiently specified requests the two agree: both require one Goal.

**Classification.** This is reconciling measurement to behavior the owner already accepted, not a new product acceptance change. Three conditions apply:
- It is recorded as a **versioned amendment** (for example N1-01/D029). The old row stays as a historical PASS, labeled "lexical path, immediate-Goal semantics, superseded by D029, not current Primary proof." It is never relabeled.
- No safety or fixed criterion becomes weaker. The 15/16 threshold, zero Goals on the 24 nonrequests, and the explicit explanation for unsupported sending all stay. Each is tightened where needed (see section 2).
- Expected outcomes for each case are derived from the owner's stated principle and frozen before any run.

**Noninferable human judgment.** None is needed to adopt the mapping. The behavior choice (clarify instead of creating an empty Goal) is the owner's own D029 instruction. The only real judgment is the boundary between essential and inferable for a few borderline cases. Handle this without a human gate: wherever the owner's principle doesn't decide a case, freeze the expected class as "either acceptable" rather than asking anyone. The one human judgment that does remain is N1-09's genuine-usefulness evaluation. It already exists and is still outstanding. It is not created by this reconciliation and must not be implied by it.

**Challenge.** The main regression risk under D029 is that "essential question" becomes an escape hatch. A model that always asks would score 16/16 on recognition while delegating nothing. The mapping must close this hole, not just permit questions.

## 2. Smallest defensible mapping

Keep the 40 original inputs and the old candidate results unchanged. Before the run, freeze for each of the 16 requests:

- **Expected class:**
  - `DELEGATE`: sufficient or generic draft is possible. A question here counts as a miss.
  - `ASK`: no draftable content exists.
  - `EITHER`: the principle doesn't decide.
- **Facts that must not be invented.** For example, in 「まだ日付は決まっていませんが、招待文の下書きを作って。」 the user says the date is deliberately undecided:
  - Asking for the date is a miss.
  - A fabricated date is a miss.
  - The allowed outcomes are a Goal with a date placeholder, or a question about other missing essentials (such as the event) that does not ask for the date.
- **Questions that count as non-essential.** Under D028, approval questions such as "Shall I draft this?" or confirmation of facts already given are misses.

**Scoring for each request**, reported in three separate buckets (delegated, clarified, miss):
- **Delegated:** exactly one Goal on turn 1, in an allowed class, plus a real artifact grounded in the supplied facts with no invented specifics.
- **Clarified:** zero Goals on turn 1 and one grouped, essential question, in an allowed class. Then, after the frozen natural answer, exactly one Goal in the same conversation and a grounded real artifact. A question with no successful follow-through counts as a **miss**, not as recognition.
- **Miss:** anything else, including errors, timeouts, refusals, wrong Goal counts, non-essential or approval questions, fabricated facts, and unsupported effects.

**Thresholds:**
- Requests: at least 15/16 delegated or clarified. This keeps the original threshold.
- Nonrequests: zero Goals across all turns. Unsupported sending must be explicitly explained, and no turn may imply sending is supported.
- All 40: zero unsupported or external effects. Host validation of IDs, snapshots, revision and epoch must hold for every commit.

"Grounded useful downstream output" is limited to checks a machine can verify: supplied facts are present, no fabricated specifics, and the artifact is real. Genuine usefulness stays under N1-09's human evaluation and is not claimed here.

N1-02 needs no remapping. The independently frozen D029 heldout12 two-turn run is the current-Primary evidence for it. The lexical PASS stays historical.

**Scope:** exactly the 40 frozen inputs, plus one frozen natural answer turn for each request case. The answer is used only if the model asks. Nothing else is added.

**Frozen before the run, then hashed and recorded:**

**Cohorts:** stay inside the existing limit of at most 24 first attempts and 900 seconds per cohort. Count answer turns as calls, so the budget isn't expanded silently.
